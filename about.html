Part 1 · Born of Tafiti99

Our Story

Tafiti99 was born from a simple observation: students know the theory, but struggle with the practice.

The idea was drafted on November 7th, 2025, during the holiday break. Sadick A. Tunda (MD5), who is passionate about data analysis, digital systems and writing, discussed it with his colleague Laurent Chandi (currently an intern doctor). Laurent had been consulting privately for years and had been thinking about initiating a research consultation firm.

Laurent gave Sadick the green light. Sadick combined his technical web-development skills with his knowledge of medical research to create a service that is both fast and high quality.
